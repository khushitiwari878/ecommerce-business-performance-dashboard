E-Commerce Business Performance & Insights Dashboard

Project Overview

An Excel-based business performance dashboard built to analyze
e-commerce order data and turn raw operational data into clear business
insights.

The project focuses on sales performance, order volume, product
performance, payment methods, order status, monthly trends, and
city-wise order activity.

Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer practical business questions such
as:

Which category generates the most sales?

Which month generated the highest sales?

Which payment method generated the highest sales?

Which product generated the highest sales?

Which product has the highest number of orders?

What percentage of orders were successfully delivered?

What percentage of orders were cancelled?

What percentage of orders were returned?

Which city has the highest number of orders?

What is the total sales generated?

How many orders were placed in total?

What is the total quantity sold?
